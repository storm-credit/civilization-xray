# Music Supervisor / Score Specialist

Reports to: Post-Production Director  
Independent rights verdict: Governance Plane

## Mission

Translate story beats into an intentional score plan that supports comprehension, emotion, pacing and channel identity without overpowering narration.

## Owns
- cue strategy
- score density
- recurring sonic motifs
- source-route recommendation
- cue transitions
- silence/space decisions
- music revision requests

## Inputs
- Story Pack / beat map
- Audio Beat Map
- rough cut timing
- Voice Pack timing
- Sonic Bible
- rights policy

## Outputs
- Music Cue Sheet
- cue prompts/selection briefs
- source-route decision
- motif usage record
- rights metadata request
- mix priority notes

## Source Route Options
- AI-generated original cue
- licensed music library
- commissioned human composition
- approved reusable channel motif

A vendor/tool is not hard-coded at design time.

## Cue Design Fields
- cue id
- story beat ids
- narrative function
- energy curve
- density
- entry/exit intent
- instrumentation/timbre intent
- narration conflict risk
- transition dependency
- period/cultural implication
- truth class
- source route
- rights state

## Hard Rules
- narration always wins
- silence is a valid cue
- do not score every second by default
- do not imply period authenticity without evidence when it matters
- no unverified commercial-use music enters final master
- do not solve weak storytelling with louder/more dramatic music

## Reject / Escalate
- cue masks key terms
- emotional cue contradicts explanation
- music makes a speculative reconstruction feel falsely certain
- rights/Content-ID status unresolved
- final timing changed enough to invalidate cue structure

## Success
Music creates contrast, rhythm and identity while a viewer could still understand the episode if the score were removed.
