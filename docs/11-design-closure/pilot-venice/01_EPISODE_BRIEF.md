## Selected Central Question
> **연약한 석호 지반 위에 무거운 건물을 세우기 위해 베네치아의 건축가들은 지반과 기초를 어떻게 구성했는가?**

## Viewer Promise
수면 아래를 단면으로 열어, 대표적인 베네치아 목재 말뚝 기초에서 **연약한 토층 → 조밀한 말뚝 → 목재 수평층/기초 → 하중벽**의 관계가 어떻게 작동하는지 보여준다. 동시에 “물속 나무는 영원히 썩지 않는다” 같은 대중적 단순화를 교정한다.

## Episode Grammar
MYSTERY → MECHANISM → QUALIFIER → REASSEMBLY

Primary grammar: Mechanism Mystery

## Target Audience
- 한국 일반 지식 시청자
- 토목/건축 전공지식 없음
- 구조공학 수식 없이 이해 가능
- 역사적 분위기보다 “왜 가능한지”를 알고 싶은 시청자

VERY HIGH

## Explanatory Unit
`representative_section`

도시 전체를 하나의 동일 구조로 모델링하지 않는다.

대표 단면:
- canal/building edge context
- load-bearing masonry/wall
- foundation base / timber horizontal layer concept
- closely spaced timber piles
- representative lagoon soil layers
- waterline

## Case Anchors
Primary evidence anchor:
- **Church of Santa Maria Maggiore, Venice** — foundation-pile preservation/mechanical research case

Secondary evidence anchor:
- **Rialto Bridge** — separately studied wooden foundation case, useful to demonstrate site-specific variation and long-lived waterlogged foundation material

Rule:
Primary case evidence를 베네치아 전체에 자동 일반화하지 않는다.

## Scope In
- lagoon soft-soil problem
- closely spaced wooden pile ground/foundation reinforcement in documented Venetian cases
- representative foundation section
- role of horizontal timber/planking/deck where supported
- load path at conceptual level
- waterlogged wood preservation/decay nuance
- site/era variability
- why representative reconstruction must be labeled

## Scope Out
- MOSE flood barriers
- climate change/sea-level rise deep dive
- full history of Venice
- every Venetian building foundation typology
- exact foundation of St. Mark’s Basilica unless separately researched
- exact citywide pile count
- “wood mineralized into stone” claims
- detailed soil mechanics equations
- exact construction sequence not supported by sources

## Candidate Viewer Misconceptions
1. Venice literally floats on water.
2. Every building stands on identical piles of identical depth/species.
3. The piles always reach a deep hard layer.
4. Waterlogged wood is perfectly preserved because “no oxygen = no decay.”
5. Wooden piles alone carry the whole explanation; soil reinforcement/load distribution do not matter.

## Early Fact Risks
- building/site variation collapsed into one universal diagram
- preservation myth stated too strongly
- short-pile ground improvement vs deeper bearing-pile descriptions mixed without site qualifier
- decorative historical reconstruction mistaken for exact evidence
- sampled wood species generalized to all Venice
- settlement/flooding conflated with foundation collapse

## Production Risks
- city-scale scene too complex before mechanism is clear
- camera descent loses orientation
- soil layers shown with fake geological precision
- pile density/spacing visually appears exact without evidence
- Veo historical worker scene changes foundation topology
- excessive “epic ancient secret” music undermines documentary tone

## Success Criteria
1. First 30 seconds establishes Venice + hidden below-ground problem + visual transformation.
2. Viewer can state after viewing: “대표적인 경우 조밀한 목재 말뚝과 상부 기초가 연약 지반을 보강/지지하는 시스템의 일부였다.”
3. Viewer is not left with “all Venice uses identical piles.”
4. Viewer is not told “submerged wood never decays.”
5. Central mechanism is primarily proven with deterministic section/3D, not generative atmosphere.
6. Historical reconstruction is visually distinguished from documented geometry.
7. Final payoff returns to the original city view with the below-ground structure mentally visible.

## Stop Conditions
Do not advance if research cannot support:
- a representative Venetian wooden-pile foundation arrangement
- clear scope limitations
- a defensible explanation of preservation/decay nuance

Do not invent missing internal details to make the 3D prettier.
