## BS-F02 — Channel identity collapses into generic AI-history / cinematic-history content

Severity: **CRITICAL / market identity**

Observed risk:
- AI history reconstruction and time-travel/cinematic formats are increasingly easy to reproduce;
- SH09-style historical atmosphere can become more memorable than the hidden mechanism.

Existing controls:
- `History is a context/pillar. Mechanism is the channel identity.`
- every topic must answer the X-Ray hidden-mechanism question;
- SH09 is optional, one-action, non-authoritative, and cuttable;
- title/thumbnail cannot promise generic history spectacle when the episode thesis is engineering/system explanation.

Decision: **KEEP**.

Residual risk:
- actual audience may still categorize the channel incorrectly from thumbnails or early episodes.

Empirical trigger:
- repeated comments/CTR/retention indicate viewers primarily expect historical spectacle rather than mechanism explanation.

Recovery:
- patch packaging/episode mix before changing umbrella identity.

## BS-F03 — Generative workbench becomes a shadow Story/Visual/Post authority

Severity: **CRITICAL / authority**

Observed risk:
- AniJam can automatically decompose scripts into scenes, propose camera choices and assemble timelines.

Existing controls:
- whole-script workbench output is `NON-CANON`;
- canonical chain remains Evidence → Story → Visual Plan → Production Pack;
- workbench receives approved Visual Work Orders by default;
- final timeline remains Audio/Post-owned;
- Blender/2D remain factual spatial authority.

Decision: **KEEP**.

Reopen trigger:
- a future tool requires persistent state that cannot be represented in P6/P7 without authority ambiguity.

## BS-F04 — Rights-cleared material is mistaken for factual evidence

Severity: **CRITICAL / trust**

Observed risk:
- open-license or Public Domain imagery can look authoritative even when it is a later illustration or context image.

Existing controls:
- `rights-cleared ≠ evidence-authoritative`;
- 1902 Campanile foundation drawing is named-case only;
- modern creator-made Venice construction illustration is rejected as factual evidence;
- Evidence Pack source hierarchy remains factual authority.

Decision: **KEEP**.

Residual:

Detection:
- visual QA asks whether a reasonable viewer would infer more certainty than Evidence Pack supports.
